The Chemistry and Science of Stubborn Odors

Odors are volatile organic compounds that our nose detects as they travel through air. Biohazard odors originate from bacterial decomposition, bodily fluid breakdown, and chemical reactions in decaying organic matter. These odor-causing compounds do not remain suspended in air. They settle into porous materials and become trapped. Carpet padding absorbs and holds odor molecules. Subflooring and wall cavities trap odors deep in the home's structure. Insulation becomes saturated with odor-producing compounds. Simple surface cleaning does not reach these locations. The odor continues because the source remains embedded. Professional deodorization must penetrate porous materials and neutralize the compounds creating the smell at a molecular level.
