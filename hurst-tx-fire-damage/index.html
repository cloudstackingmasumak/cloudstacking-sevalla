Understanding Your Insurance Claim

Your insurance policy determines coverage and out-of-pocket costs. Homeowners policies typically cover fire damage as a covered peril. Deductibles apply to fire damage claims, reducing your reimbursement. Coverage limits cap what insurance pays for restoration, potentially leaving gaps. Contents coverage reimburses personal property damage up to policy limits. Additional living expenses coverage helps with temporary housing if your home is uninhabitable. Replacement cost coverage reimburses actual restoration costs without depreciation. Actual cash value coverage deducts depreciation, reimbursing less than replacement costs. Subrogation rights allow insurers to recover from contractors responsible for fire. Reviewing your policy before fire damage helps you understand coverage. Contact your insurer immediately after fire to report the claim and start the process. Adjusters visit to document damage and authorize restoration work. Understanding your coverage helps you plan for costs that insurance does not cover in Hurst, TX.
