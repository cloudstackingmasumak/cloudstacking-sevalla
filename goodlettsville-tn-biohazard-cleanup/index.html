What Is an Unattended Death

An unattended death occurs when someone passes away and isn't discovered by another person for a period of time, potentially ranging from days to weeks or longer depending on circumstances. This includes deaths in homes, vehicles, rental properties, or commercial spaces where the body remains undiscovered. Unattended deaths differ significantly from deaths where medical professionals are present or where family members find the person shortly after passing. The time that elapses between death and discovery determines how far decomposition has progressed and what biological hazards are present in the environment.
