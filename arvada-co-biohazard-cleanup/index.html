Real Occupational Hazards First Responders Face

First responders in Arvada are trained to manage acute emergencies, but extended exposure to biohazardous materials carries cumulative health consequences that can affect responders for years or even a lifetime. Mold in abandoned or neglected spaces releases mycotoxins and dangerous spores that damage respiratory systems over time. Sewage and waste harbor dangerous bacteria like E. coli and Listeria that cause serious illness. Responders may have minor cuts or abrasions that go unnoticed during a call. Viruses and bacteria exploit these small breaks in the skin.
