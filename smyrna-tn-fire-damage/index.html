Furniture Restoration Details

Burned upholstered furniture goes to specialized shops where it's assessed. Minor damage might need only cleaning. Moderate damage might require replacing cushions or reupholstering sections. Severe damage might mean the frame is salvaged but cushions and coverings are completely replaced.

This is why you don't throw out your burned couch. A professional assessment might show that cleaning plus new cushions or a full reupholster is worth the cost and preserves a beloved piece of furniture.

Wooden furniture is similarly assessed. Deep cleaning removes soot. Sanding and refinishing restore finish and appearance. Structural damage to joints or legs is repaired by furniture specialists.

Soft contents like clothing, bedding, and upholstery go through specialized cleaning processes. Dry cleaning methods work for some items. Wet cleaning in industrial washers handles others. Deodorization treatments remove smoke odor from fabric.

Hard surfaces like glassware, dishes, and picture frames are hand-cleaned or run through industrial washers. Depending on soot severity, multiple cleaning cycles may be needed. Delicate items require careful handling to prevent breakage.

Electronics like televisions, computers, and stereos are power-cleaned and inspected before return. Some may need component replacement. Restoration technicians test electronics to ensure they work safely.
