Biohazard waste encompasses many different categories of contaminated materials that require specialized disposal in Longmont. Infectious waste includes materials contaminated with bloodborne pathogens including blood-soaked gauze, contaminated clothing, and bodily fluid containers. Sharps including needles, scalpels, and other sharp objects that could cause injury are placed in puncture-proof containers. Pharmaceutical waste including expired medications, syringes with residual drugs, and contaminated pharmaceutical materials. Animal waste from hoarding situations and pest control operations. Collection and Segregation Procedures

Proper collection and segregation of biohazard waste begins at the site where contamination occurs in Longmont. We use appropriate containers for each waste category, preventing cross-contamination between different material types. Sharps are placed in rigid, puncture-proof containers labeled with biohazard symbols. Liquid waste and fluids are placed in leak-proof containers to prevent spillage and contamination. Solid contaminated materials are placed in leak-proof plastic bags clearly labeled as biohazard waste. All containers are sealed before transport to prevent exposure during handling. We use color-coded bags and containers following standard biohazard waste management protocols. Segregation ensures that different waste types are disposed of using appropriate methods rather than treating all biohazard waste identically.
