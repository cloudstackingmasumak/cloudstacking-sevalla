Pets with open wounds or compromised immune systems face even greater danger.

Pet Safety and the Risk of Disease Transmission

Pets in your Medford home face serious health risks from biohazard contamination. Cats and dogs naturally investigate their environment by sniffing and touching surfaces with their noses and paws. They may walk through contaminated areas and later lick their paws or groom themselves, ingesting pathogens. Pets may also lie on contaminated surfaces, allowing pathogens to make contact with their skin and potentially enter their bodies. In Medford, OR, pets can become carriers of bloodborne pathogens and other diseases without showing symptoms themselves. They then transmit these pathogens to your family members through casual contact, licking, or sharing spaces. Your pets cannot understand the danger or protect themselves from contamination. They depend on you to ensure their environment is safe and clean. Professional biohazard remediation protects your pets by eliminating the sources of contamination completely.

Dogs and cats naturally investigate their environments through touching and sniffing contaminated areas. Pets may eat contaminated materials or lick surfaces without understanding the danger. Their smaller body size means exposure that seems minor to an adult can create serious health consequences for your pet. In Medford, OR, veterinarians see increased illness cases when families are dealing with contaminated properties.

Your beloved pets depend on you to provide a safe home where they can play and relax without health risks.
