How Smoke Travels Through Homes

Smoke does not simply rise and disappear. It flows like water through your home following paths of least resistance. Warm smoke rises until it hits the ceiling, then spreads horizontally in all directions. As smoke cools, it descends affecting lower levels throughout the house. In Indianapolis, smoke flows up stairwells, down hallways, and through any opening or gap. It follows air currents created by heating and cooling systems. Closed interior doors slow but do not stop smoke penetration. The differential air pressure inside versus outside rooms pushes smoke through tiny cracks. This infiltration happens within minutes of the fire starting. By the time firefighters control flames, smoke has already contaminated numerous rooms. Understanding these flow patterns explains why living rooms show damage even when fire started in a garage. Homes in IN experience smoke traveling upward through walls and into attics.
